# Design. Decision Trade-offs

Almost every decision is a trade-off

1. Performance vs. Cost
2. Security vs. Flexibility
3. Reliability vs. Agility
4. Scalability vs. Cost or Reliability
5. etc

## Buy vs. Build

Why Buy|Why Build
-|-
Use a potentianlly better product from specialized company|More flexibility and control over the feature set and caabilities
Reduce management and operational costs|More control over the development lifecycle
Use mature and tested products|Better compatibility and integration with internal systems
Predictable costs using pay-per-use model|High costs at the beginning with optimized costs during operational phase

Example:
- Running containers:
  - "Buy": Google Kubernetes Engine
  - "Build": self-developed orchestration

## Modify vs. Deprecate

When to Modify|When to Deprecate
-|-
Features can be extended to include desired new features|No longer adding value
Existing in-house expertise|Another product exists
When ROI justfies Modification (good value, low cost, etc)|Legacy programming language or frameworks, hard to maintain
